Free font for personal and commercial use.

Made by Evgeny Koroletov, Ukraine (koroletov@gmail.com)

The typeface was created to support independent comic and manga artists working with Cyrillic-based languages.
Originally designed for the comic series TroubleSide by Lugansk Contemporary Diaspora (a community of displaced artists from Donbass, Ukraine), instagram.com/lkd_dragon.

Inspired by Comicraft fonts and classic American comic book aesthetics.
TroubleSide supports over 35 Latin- and Cyrillic-based languages, including English, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Russian, Polish, Romanian, French, German, Spanish, and many more. The font includes uppercase and alternate uppercase characters, diacritics, punctuation, and essential symbols. It also features custom kerning for smoother, more balanced text composition.
